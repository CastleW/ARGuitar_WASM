🛠️ Quick 3-Step Assembly Guide:
  1. Print at 100%: Ensure printer scale is set to 100% (Actual Size). Measure the 50mm blue scale check bar on each sheet with a ruler to verify accuracy.
  2. Cut & Align Overlaps: Cut along the red dashed lines on Sheet 2 and Sheet 3. Place them over the shaded overlap tabs on Sheet 1 and Sheet 2, aligning the red crosshairs.
  3. Mount on Card or Wood: Glue or tape the assembled 64cm strip onto a 45–46mm wide piece of wood moulding, heavy card, or a yardstick/ruler. Launch ARGuitar and play!
